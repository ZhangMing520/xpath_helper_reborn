Privacy Policy — XPath Helper Reborn

中文 / English

English

Last updated: 2026-10-01

XPath Helper Reborn is a browser extension that lets you write, edit, and evaluate XPath queries against the web page you are currently viewing. Press the shortcut or click the toolbar button to open the bar; hold Shift while hovering an element to inspect it.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not send any data to any remote server.

Permissions

The extension requests the <all_urls> host permission because its core function — evaluating an XPath expression that you type against the page you are inspecting — requires reading that page's DOM. This permission is used only locally and on demand. The extension does not read your pages in the background, and it never reads pages you have not actively inspected.

Local processing

All XPath evaluation happens entirely on your device, inside the page you are inspecting. Query results are displayed only in the extension's own toolbar and are never uploaded anywhere.

Third parties

There are no third parties. The extension contains no code that contacts external servers, and it integrates with no external services.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/ZhangMing520/xpath_helper_reborn.

中文

最后更新：2026-10-01

XPath Helper Reborn 是一款浏览器扩展，用于在您当前浏览的网页上编写、编辑并运行 XPath 查询。按下快捷键或点击工具栏按钮即可打开工具栏；按住 Shift 并悬停某个元素即可查看它。

我们不收集的数据

  • 我们不收集、传输或存储任何个人信息。
  • 我们不使用任何分析、广告或第三方追踪 SDK。
  • 我们不会向任何远程服务器发送任何数据。

权限说明

本扩展申请 <all_urls> 主机权限，因为其核心功能——对您输入的 XPath 表达式在当前页面上求值——需要在您选择查看的任意网站上读取页面 DOM。该权限仅在本机、按需使用； 扩展不会在后台读取您的页面，也绝不会读取您未主动查看的页面。

本地处理

所有 XPath 求值都完全在您设备的当前页面内完成。查询结果仅显示在本扩展自带的工具栏中， 绝不上传至任何地方。

第三方

不存在任何第三方。本扩展不含任何连接外部服务器的代码，也不集成任何外部服务。

联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请在 github.com/ZhangMing520/xpath_helper_reborn 提交 issue。
